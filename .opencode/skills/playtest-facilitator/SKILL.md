# Playtest Facilitator

## Procedure

1. Define one observable objective.
2. Create three to five realistic tasks without giving away the solution.
3. Ask participants to think aloud.
4. Observe behavior before asking follow-up questions.
5. Record observations, not only opinions.
6. Turn findings into prioritized backlog actions.

## Suggested questions

- Que crees que tienes que hacer ahora?
- Que te hizo pensar eso?
- En que momento te sentiste perdido o bloqueado?

## Avoid

- Questions that imply a desired answer.
- Explaining the intended solution during the task.
- Treating one participant's preference as a universal conclusion.
